LaterYou Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026. Operator: Gor Manukyan. Contact: gmanuky@gmail.com

LaterYou (“the app”) is a personal portrait ritual on iPhone. There is no LaterYou server that stores your photos. This policy describes what stays on your device, what Apple and optional services may process, and how in-app purchases work.

Photos and camera

Portraits you take in LaterYou are stored in the app’s private folder on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to a LaterYou backend. They are not added to Apple Photos unless you export a photo or a timelapse. The camera is used only when you take a portrait. Live preview and face alignment run on-device using Apple’s Vision framework. Face landmarks never leave the phone as a network upload from LaterYou.

Reminders and lock

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. LaterYou does not send push messages from a server. If you enable Face ID, iOS handles biometrics; LaterYou does not store Face ID data.

iCloud backup (optional, LaterYou Pro)

If you turn on iCloud backup, series names and portrait files copy into the private CloudKit database tied to your Apple ID (container iCloud.com.lateryou.app). That data is not a LaterYou-operated cloud. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy apply. You can also export a LaterYou backup file on this iPhone without using iCloud.

LaterYou Pro and payments

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. LaterYou never sees your card number. LaterYou Pro yearly is an auto-renewing subscription (USD 29.99 per year after a 7-day free trial, or the equivalent in your App Store region). Lifetime is a one-time purchase (USD 79.99, or regional equivalent). Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

To know whether Pro is active (extra series, iCloud restore, timelapse without an end card), the app uses RevenueCat, which receives App Store purchase and subscription status, anonymous app user identifiers, and device/app metadata needed to validate receipts. It does not receive your portraits. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

LaterYou does not use third-party advertising networks and does not track you across other companies’ apps for advertising. The app does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt for ads. Crash or usage analytics are not required for the core ritual; if that changes, this policy will be updated.

Children

You may create a series of a child you are responsible for. Those portraits are stored like any other series (on device, and in your iCloud if you enable backup). Do not publish a child’s portraits from LaterYou without a lawful basis. The app is not directed at children creating their own Apple ID accounts.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting a photo or a series removes it from the app on that iPhone. If iCloud backup is on, corresponding CloudKit records are requested to be deleted. Deleting the app removes local files; iCloud copies remain in your Apple ID until you delete them via the app while signed in, or via Apple’s iCloud tools. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription; cancel in Apple ID settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, and California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. For portraits, you already control the files on device and in your iCloud. For purchase records, contact Apple as well. Email gmanuky@gmail.com for privacy requests.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use of LaterYou after an update means you accept the revised policy.
